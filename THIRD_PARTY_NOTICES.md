## ANSI Love artwork

Warpus of Legacy Krew drew “ansi love” in December 2019. Source:
<https://www.ansilove.org/assets/examples/wa-ansilove.ans>.
SHA-256: `5c5d75b205680a1098517ef36432ce36161415c2dd2f9552b9ede7cdc2330ce3`.

The sysc-Go dependency embeds the original ANSI source and its VGA raster.
The renderer resamples the drawing, adds motion, and retains the on-screen
credit “Drawn by Warpus, December 2019”. The source does not include an
explicit redistribution licence; this notice does not grant one or apply
the project's code licence to the artwork.
